Freefall Gauntlet: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Freefall Gauntlet is a sky-jumping game for Meta Quest headsets. This page explains what the game does with your information. The short version: the game does not collect, store on a server, share or sell your personal information.

What stays on your headset

The game saves your progress (best scores, stars, unlocked levels) and your options (volume, voice sounds, comfort vignette, snap-turn angle) on your headset. This data never leaves the device and is removed when you uninstall the game.

What the game does not do

  • It does not use your microphone, camera or location.
  • It does not show ads and has no analytics or tracking of its own.
  • It does not ask for your name, email address or any account details.
  • It does not send your body movement or controller data anywhere. Head and hand positions are used only on the headset, while you play, to steer your character.

Meta platform

When the game starts it asks the Meta Horizon platform whether your Meta account owns the game. Meta answers yes or no; the game does not receive or keep your name, email or other profile details. Meta processes this check, and the other data it collects when you use your headset and the Meta Horizon Store, under its own terms and privacy policy, which you can read at meta.com/legal/privacy-policy.

Children

The game collects no personal information from anyone, including children. Meta sets the minimum age for using Meta Quest headsets.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
